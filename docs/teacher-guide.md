Written for: teachers who use the system to take attendance, enter grades and keep records.

# Teacher guide

Your sidebar shows the classes you teach and the modules you can use. You can only see and change data for your own classes.

## Your classes

**My Classes** lists the classes you teach. Open one to see its students, timetable and gradebooks.

**Workload** shows how your teaching load is spread across the week.

## Timetable and calendar

- **Timetable** shows your weekly periods.
- **Calendar** shows events and important dates.

## Taking attendance

1. Open **Attendance** and choose your class, or go straight to the roll.
2. Mark each student present, absent, late or excused, then click **Save**. If the connection drops, the roll is kept on the device and sent when you are back online.
3. If your school uses attendance codes, students can enter the code for the lesson on their own device. Codes expire.

When attendance is saved, the system checks for truancy. If a student now meets the alert threshold (3 consecutive or 10 or more unexcused absences), an alert goes to the office. You do not need to do anything extra.

## Grades

1. Open **Grades** and choose the class and assessment.
2. Enter a score for each student. A score cannot be higher than the assessment's maximum.
3. Letter grades and class averages are calculated for you.

You can only enter grades for classes you teach. Grade edits are recorded in the audit log.

## Behaviour

- Log an incident from **Behaviour** if a student's conduct needs recording. Choose the severity, and add a note the office can follow up on.
- You can see incidents you raised. Suspensions and parent notifications are handled by admins.

## Messages and notices

- **Communication** shows notices posted for your role or year levels.
- You can message parents and staff in the ways the school allows. Messages are kept in the thread for reference.

## Good practice

- Take attendance at the start of each lesson, while it is fresh.
- Enter grades soon after marking. Late edits are logged, which helps if a grade is queried.
- Do not share your login. If you think someone else has used your account, tell an admin so the password can be reset.
- If something looks wrong (a missing class or a wrong score), tell an admin rather than trying to work around it.
